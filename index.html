02 Writing

Teaching it back

I write series on Medium that rebuild ideas from the ground up. Every article is paired with clean, runnable code, collected in one repository, each series with its own structure and isolated dependencies.

🧠

Neural Networks Demystified

A step-by-step journey into how neural networks really work under the hood, implemented entirely in NumPy. The goal is to remove the magic from deep learning and rebuild it with pure math and simple code.

  • Neurons, perceptrons and vectorized forward passes
  • Activation and loss functions
  • Backpropagation derived and implemented step-by-step
  • Dense layers, initialization and training loops
  • A NumPy-only MNIST classifier
Read on Medium ↗ Code ↗
🔒

The Art of Secrecy

A hands-on exploration of classical and modern cryptographic systems. Every article comes with a clean Python implementation that shows how the cipher actually works, rather than describing it from a distance.

  • Caesar, Vigenère and substitution ciphers
  • A full, modular Enigma machine simulation
  • Rotors with ring settings and turnover positions
  • Reflectors and plugboard
  • Custom rotor orders and a complete encrypt/decrypt pipeline

04 About

A mechanical engineer who fell for gradients

I work as a data scientist and machine learning engineer, specialising specifically in deep learning. I'm studying Mechanical Engineering at the University of Lagos, which is where the habit of taking things apart to see how they work came from, and it just ended up pointed at neural networks.

Most of what I build starts from scratch, because implementing a thing is the only way I trust that I understand it. That instinct runs from a NumPy-only deep learning framework to a bitboard chess engine, and it's why I write about the internals as much as I build them.

Right now I'm working toward becoming a serious chess programmer. The Hexapawn agent was the warm-up, and the engine is the real thing.
